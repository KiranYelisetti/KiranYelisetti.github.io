Bangle Bazaar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Bangle Bazaar is a puzzle game made by Ravi Kiran Yelisetti, an independent developer in India. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your phone

Your level, streak, settings and play history are saved on your own device so you can continue where you stopped. This information stays on your phone. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings, under Privacy, then Ads.

Analytics

The game may use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played, for example which levels are too hard. This covers anonymous usage events and device information. It does not identify you personally.

Children

Bangle Bazaar is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything the game saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
