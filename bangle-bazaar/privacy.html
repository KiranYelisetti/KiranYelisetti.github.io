Bangle Bazaar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Bangle Bazaar is a puzzle game made by Ravi Kiran Yelisetti, an independent developer in India. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores on your phone

Your saved puzzle, level, streak, resources and settings are stored on your device so you can continue where you stopped. The game also keeps a limited local gameplay event history for diagnostics. The game has no accounts and does not request your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Some gameplay events may be sent to Google as described below.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads and ads between some levels through Google AdMob. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions, and performance and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure results and prevent fraud. These identifiers can link activity to a device; the data is not necessarily anonymous. Read how Google uses information from partner apps.

The app checks Google's consent service before requesting ads and presents applicable consent forms. Open Privacy in the game, then Ad privacy choices when available, to review or change those choices. If the consent check fails, ads stay unavailable while the game remains playable.

On iPhone, tracking permission is requested separately when needed. You can change it in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from playing. Android advertising ID controls are in Android's Privacy and Ads settings.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand gameplay, such as level starts and completions, moves, hints and ad outcomes. Analytics can include app-instance identifiers, device and app information, usage events and approximate location derived from IP addresses. It is not described as anonymous.

On iPhone, gameplay analytics is off by default. You can enable Share gameplay analytics in the game's Privacy menu and disable it there at any time. The iPhone analytics configuration excludes advertising ID support and disables analytics advertising personalization. Android analytics currently uses its existing collection settings. Advertising SDK measurement is separate from the optional gameplay analytics setting.

Children

Bangle Bazaar is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

You can remove local game data by deleting the app and its data, or by clearing app storage on Android. Offloading an iPhone app can retain its data. Turning off iPhone gameplay analytics stops future gameplay analytics collection and resets the local analytics identifier; it does not erase data already sent to Google. For questions or requests about information already collected, contact us below. Google handles service data under its own privacy and retention policies.
